ICIMOD / IWRA — Transboundary Water Governance in the HKH Region

The intelligence gap is structural, not incidental

Eight countries share the Hindu Kush Himalayan system. Each maintains fragmented monitoring networks, operates under different data regimes, and lacks access to integrated regional intelligence. The consequence is predictable: climate finance is misallocated, infrastructure fails without warning, and disaster response lags weeks behind events.

Dristhi Earth is built to close this gap — aggregating, analysing, and translating environmental data into structured intelligence for the decision-makers who need it most.
